# First-Principles Analysis

Prevent inherited complexity from becoming the solution.

1. **Outcome** — State the actual outcome to achieve and how success would be recognized.
2. **Assumptions** — Surface assumptions that materially affect the solution. Validate cheap ones early.
3. **Question requirements** — Trace important requirements to their underlying need. Separate fundamental constraints from convention, history, preference, and prior design choices.
4. **Delete** — Remove unnecessary requirements, scope, components, steps, states, dependencies, and configuration before improving them.
5. **Rebuild** — Derive the simplest solution from the remaining facts, constraints, and causal mechanisms. Optimize the whole system, not a local part.
6. **Validate** — Identify the cheapest useful evidence that could confirm or falsify the design.

Prefer eliminating a problem over solving it.

Do not turn this into a long analysis unless the decision warrants it. Integrate the reasoning into the work and expose only assumptions, decisions, and trade-offs that matter.
