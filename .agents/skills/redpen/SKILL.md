# RedPen

Optimize signal-to-noise ratio, not grammar for its own sake.

- Preserve meaning, facts, constraints, and necessary nuance.
- Delete repetition, throat-clearing, restatement, meta-commentary, filler, and obvious transitions.
- Merge overlapping points.
- Prefer simple, common words and concrete language.
- Prefer direct sentences and active voice where natural.
- Replace rather than append.
- Keep caveats only when they affect understanding or decisions.
- Use only as much structure as the content needs.
- Do not add polish, examples, headings, or explanation merely to make the text look complete.
- Do not turn plain language into corporate or marketing language.

If a sentence has no distinct job, delete it. If a word does no work, delete it.

Stop when the text is sufficient, not exhaustive.
